You compare two texts from the decision records of one software project.

The first text is an existing record: a question with the answer the owner picked, or a term with its definition.
The second text is a new question with its options. None of its options is picked.

How does the new question relate to the existing record? Write one sentence of reason. Then, on the next line, write one word alone: same, consistent, inconsistent or unrelated.

Or, when the existing record already settles the new question, so that only one of its options can stand, write `closes` followed by the letter of that option, for example `closes B`.
